Anime India Hyderabad 2026: The Ultimate Visitor Guide

Anime and Japanese pop-culture fans in Telangana and across India are gearing up for Anime India Hyderabad 2026. Billed as India's premier anime celebration, the event promises multi-stage programming, exclusive merchandise, and authentic Japanese cultural exhibits.

📅 Dates: Saturday 14 & Sunday 15 November 2026
⏰ Time: From 10:00 AM onwards
📍 Venue: The Address – Conventions & Exhibitions, Hyderabad
🎟️ Official Tickets: Starting from ₹499 (Available on District)
🏢 Organizer: Animation Xpress India Pvt Ltd
🎟️ Book Official Tickets on District

Key Attractions & What to Expect

  • VIP Guests from Japan: Special industry guests, animators, and talent visiting directly from Tokyo.
  • Three Programming Stages: Non-stop panel talks, industry announcements, live musical performances, and creator Q&As.
  • Cosplay Championship & Zone: A dedicated showcase area for competitive and casual cosplayers.
  • Maid & Butler Café: An experiential dining pop-up inspired by Akihabara culture.
  • Official Merch & Fan Alley: Official Japanese merchandise alongside independent creator booths.

Helpful Japanese Phrases to Use at the Convention

If you're planning to interact with Japanese guests or fellow otaku, try these authentic phrases:

Phrase (Japanese) Romaji Meaning
はじめまして Hajimemashite Nice to meet you
写真を撮ってもいいですか？ Shashin o totte mo ii desu ka? May I take your photo? (Crucial for cosplayers)
コスプレすごいです！ Kosupure sugoi desu! Your cosplay is amazing!
ありがとうございます Arigatou gozaimasu Thank you very much
