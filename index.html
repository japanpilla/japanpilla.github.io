Going for the first time?

Our Anime India visitor guide has first-timer tips, cosplay etiquette and a list of Japanese phrases to use when you meet fans and guests. Short on time? These are the three things to do first:

  1. Book early and keep a screenshot of your ticket on your phone.
  2. Plan your two days. Pick a few panels or performances, then explore the expo floor.
  3. Learn five phrases. Start with convention Japanese and impress the guests.
