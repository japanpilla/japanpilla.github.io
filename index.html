About the Fest

Get ready, anime fans! The biggest celebration of Japanese culture in India is coming to Hyderabad. Anime India is more than an expo – it's an immersive celebration with full-day programming across three stages and non-stop experiences across a huge expo floor. Whether you're a seasoned otaku or just starting your anime journey, there's something for you.
