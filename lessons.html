Japanese Lessons in Telugu

By japanpilla, a Telugu creator based in Tokyo · Updated October 2026

These lessons are written for Telugu speakers, using Telugu explanations and comparisons that English-based courses cannot give you. They focus on Japanese you can use on your first week in Japan, at a convention, or while watching anime.

Recommended order

  1. Japanese for Telugu speakers – learn how the language works and read your first hiragana.
  2. Everyday Japanese – greetings, thanks, apologies, konbini.
  3. Numbers and prices – counting and paying.
  4. Restaurant Japanese – ordering and dietary needs.
  5. Trains and stations – getting around.
  6. Anime phrases – fun vocabulary and fan culture.
