Anime Japanese vs. Real Tokyo Japanese

Japanese spoken in anime is often informal and stylized for high drama. Addressing strangers using pronouns heard in anime like Omae can sound impolite in daily conversations in Tokyo. Always use courteous honorific suffixes like -san when conversing with native speakers.
